A tiny app to block calls from non-contacts without affecting voicemail. Blocked calls are still recorded in the system call log but some phone apps won't show them. Blocking can be paused from the app or its Quick Settings tile.

<b>Options</b>

<ul><li><b>Allow repeat callers</b>: A second call from the same number within a configurable time window rings.</li><li><b>Decline instantly</b>: Callers get voicemail or a busy tone right away, instead of ringing out.</li><li><b>Silently notify</b>: Offers options to call back or add to contacts. Not shown for hidden numbers.</li></ul>

<b>Privacy</b>

This app does not request contacts, call log or internet access. Blocked numbers are only stored internally until the next incoming call after the repeat caller window expires. The statistics only store a counter, the last blocked call time, and whether it was a hidden number.

<b>Limitations</b>

<ul><li>Only one call screening app can be active at a time.</li><li>Only carrier phone calls are blocked, not ones through apps like WhatsApp or Signal.</li><li>An outgoing call does not unblock that number.</li><li>Calls are not blocked until the phone is unlocked for the first time after a reboot.</li></ul>
